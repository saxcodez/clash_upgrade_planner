Load your own export

In Clash of Clans open Settings → More Settings, scroll down to Data Export and tap Copy. Paste the copied text here. Everything stays in your browser: no login, no API key, no connection to the game.

Saved exports

Every export you analyze is saved here by date, so you can jump back and compare progress. Stored in this browser only.

To restore a backup, paste its content into the box above and tap Analyze.
